\chapter{Differences}
\label{app:differences}

\standfirst{What is different here from Pharo and Squeak, and what is
different from the 1983 system this is built on. Both lists matter, because
you will arrive from one of the two.}

\section{If you are arriving from Pharo or Squeak}

\subsection{The language}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{0.55\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
closures, \ctp{ensure:}, exceptions & present, in the \ctp{closures} dialect\\
\ct|{ }| \ct|#[ ]| block temps, pragmas & present\\
traits & present; flattened at load time, \ctp{+} composition only\\
\ctp{1.23s2} & \textbf{not implemented.} It parses as \ctp{1.23 s2}, a unary
  send, and answers \ctp{nil}\\
\ct|#(true false nil)| & three \emph{Symbols}, not the objects. Use
  \ct|{true. false. nil}|\\
\ctp{new} $\rightarrow$ \ctp{initialize} & synthesised for loaded packages and
  reported; \emph{not} automatic for a class you type into a Browser\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{The library}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{0.55\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\ct|#foo printString| & \ct|'foo'|, not \ct|'#foo'|\\
\ct|#(1 2) printString| & \ctp{'(1 2 )'}\\
\ctp{(3/4) printString} & \ctp{'(3/4)'}\\
\ct|$a value| & \ct|$a|. Use \ctp{asciiValue} or \ctp{asInteger}\\
\ctp{e description} & the class's fixed sentence, \emph{not} the message text.
  Use \ctp{messageText}\\
\ctp{255 printString: 16} & not implemented; nor is \ctp{radix:}\\
\ctp{aStream collect:} & not implemented on streams\\
\ctp{sorted:} \ctp{flatCollect:} \ctp{count:} & answer an \ctp{Array} rather
  than \ctp{self species}, except \ctp{sorted}/\ctp{sorted:} on a sequenceable
  collection\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{The environment}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{0.55\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Morphic, Bloc, Spec, Athens & none, and none planned\\
Iceberg, Metacello, Monticello & none. Packages are directories of Tonel files
  named in a profile\\
Fuel, Opal, RB-AST & none\\
\ctp{Ctrl-D} \ctp{Ctrl-P} \ctp{Ctrl-S} & none. \ctp{do it}, \ctp{print it} and
  \ctp{accept} are in the yellow-button menu\\
click-to-open menus & no: press and \emph{hold}\\
window decorations & none: no title bar, no close box. The blue button is the
  window menu\\
opening a window & choose from a menu, \emph{then} drag out a rectangle\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{The one that matters}

\begin{stwarn}
\textbf{Same-priority atomicity is gone.} Two processes at the same priority
may run simultaneously on different CPUs. Every unsynchronised
read-modify-write that was correct in Pharo is a race here.
Chapter~\ref{ch:contract} is required reading.
\end{stwarn}

\section{If you are arriving from the Blue Book}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{0.55\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
blocks & full closures. They capture variables, outlive their method, and may
  be entered concurrently\\
\ctp{\^{}} in a block & non-local return, with \ctp{cannotReturn:} when the
  home has already returned\\
\ctp{ensure:} \ctp{ifCurtailed:} & new\\
exceptions & new: \ctp{signal}, \ctp{on:do:}, \ctp{retry}, \ctp{resume:},
  \ctp{pass}. \ctp{Object>>error:} now signals a catchable \ctp{Error}\\
\ct|{ }| \ct|#[ ]| & new literal forms\\
pragmas & generalised from \ctp{<primitive: N>}\\
traits & new\\
\ctp{:=} & preferred over \ctp{\_}; both are accepted\\
\ctp{Mutex} \ctp{Monitor} \ctp{SharedQueue} \ctp{Promise} & new\\
\ctp{SharedQueue} & \emph{replaced}: the 1983 one is not safe across native
  threads\\
scheduler & M:N over native workers; same-priority processes really do run at
  once\\
the screen & grows to fill the window rather than being letterboxed\\
text & Inter, proportional and antialiased, on a one-bit display\\
files & POSIX, through \ctp{Disk} and \ctp{FileStream}\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{What is unchanged, and deliberately}

The 1983 class library, entire and unedited. The Browser, Compiler, Inspector
and Debugger are the library's own. BitBlt is the Blue Book's, byte for byte
against the Xerox traces. The bytecode semantics, the context layout, the
primitive numbering below 136, the MVC framework, the menus, the fonts
machinery, the printString formats---all 1983's.

That is not conservatism. It is what makes \ct{trace2} a usable oracle, and
\ct{trace2} is the only external check this project has on whether it is still
a Smalltalk-80.
